## Practical Tips for Pinoy Players

1. **Start Small and Scale Up:** Begin with low-tier cannons to build your confidence and learn how many shots different fish take to catch.
2. **Target the Edges:** Shoot fish right as they enter the screen. This gives your cannon maximum time to deal damage before they swim off.
